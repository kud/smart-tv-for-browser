---
title: "👋 Introduction"
description: "A smart-TV home screen that runs in the browser, driven by a D-pad, your phone or a companion extension."
---

**smart-tv-for-browser** turns any browser into a smart-TV launcher: a 10-foot
grid of streaming channels you move through with arrow keys, open with `Enter`,
and leave for the channel's own website, just as a TV hands off to an app.

Three pieces work together. The web app is the home screen itself. A phone can
pair as a remote over the internet, through a small relay. A companion browser
extension keeps that remote working once you have left smartTV for Netflix or
YouTube, and gives you a way back home.
